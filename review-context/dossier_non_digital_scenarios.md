# Operational Playbooks and Tabletop Recovery Scenarios: Degraded-Mode Operations

This document establishes the operational playbooks, tabletop recovery scenarios, and technical design requirements for degraded-mode operations within the **Pharmacy Fiduciary Commons**. It operationalizes the guidelines in [CARE_CONTINUITY.md](file:///c:/Users/Josh/Desktop/PBMRebateTreasuryFinal/CARE_CONTINUITY.md) and addresses the gates in [PRODUCTION_READINESS_CHECKLIST.md](file:///c:/Users/Josh/Desktop/PBMRebateTreasuryFinal/PRODUCTION_READINESS_CHECKLIST.md) (specifically Section 2A: Offline and Non-Digital Workflow Safety).

## 1. Executive Summary & Governance Posture

### 1.1 The Priority of Care Continuity
As established in [CARE_CONTINUITY.md:L5-7](file:///c:/Users/Josh/Desktop/PBMRebateTreasuryFinal/CARE_CONTINUITY.md#L5-7), *auditable value flow is not the same as reliable care*, and *the system may be secure while participants are unsafe*. The Commons prioritizes physical medication availability and operational liveness over automated, real-time on-chain settlement finality. When digital networks fail, the system must degrade gracefully to non-digital workflows, targeting at least a **20% Non-Digital Workflow Adoption (NDWA)** rate ([CARE_CONTINUITY.md:L47-49](file:///c:/Users/Josh/Desktop/PBMRebateTreasuryFinal/CARE_CONTINUITY.md#L47-49)).

### 1.2 Core Resilience Primitives in the Codebase
The repository contains several key offline and resilience tools designed to support this posture:
1. **`tools/resilience/continuity-engine.mjs`**: Handles offline voucher generation, schema-checking, and batch packaging.
2. **`tools/resilience/proxy-validator.js`**: Checks batched relay files for schema compliance, duplicate nullifiers, and privacy leakage.
3. **`tools/resilience/reconcile-vouchers.mjs`**: Provides a deterministic local classification algorithm for offline vouchers against on-chain records.
4. **`tools/offline/continuity-kit.html`**: A zero-dependency, self-contained HTML/JS browser utility that allows pharmacies to verify voucher MACs entirely air-gapped without remote calls or libraries.

## 2. Human Capabilities Metrics & Privacy Boundaries

To maintain operational integrity without violating participant safety, data collection for care-continuity metrics must adhere to strict privacy-safe, fraud-resistant protocols:

| Metric | Target | Collection Mechanism | Privacy Safeguards |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Continuous Refill Ratio (CRR)** | `> 95%` | Aggregated pharmacy refill variance reports submitted via trusted proxies. | No patient names, dates of birth, or specific drug names are transmitted. Data is bucketed by district code and broad ATC (Anatomical Therapeutic Chemical) classes only. |
| **Emergency Fill Access (EFA)** | `> 30 days` of credit capacity | On-chain balance queries of the zero-sum ledger in [PharmacyMutualCredit.sol](file:///c:/Users/Josh/Desktop/PBMRebateTreasuryFinal/contracts/PharmacyMutualCredit.sol). | Addresses are pseudonymous; mapping to physical pharmacies is stored strictly off-chain by local cooperatives. |
| **Non-Digital Workflow Adoption (NDWA)** | `> 20%` volume offline | Reconciliation report output from `reconcile-vouchers.mjs` comparing total local vouchers vs. total on-chain claims. | Nullifiers are cryptographic hashes (`0x` + 32-byte hex); preimages are omitted; no PII is included in the packaged batches. |
| **Medication Choice Autonomy (MCA)** | `> 90%` generic/alternative substitution rate | Advisory disputes filed with Community Juries under [CARE_CONTINUITY.md:L59-64](file:///c:/Users/Josh/Desktop/PBMRebateTreasuryFinal/CARE_CONTINUITY.md#L59-64). | Evidence metadata is hashed ([EVIDENCE_METADATA.md](file:///c:/Users/Josh/Desktop/PBMRebateTreasuryFinal/EVIDENCE_METADATA.md)); raw files containing PHI or clinical records are never uploaded to the shared ledger. |

## 3. Operational Playbook 1: Paper Vouchers

### 3.1 Playbook Procedures
Paper vouchers act as bearer recovery material enabling registered pharmacies to dispense critical medication during system outages.

1. **Voucher Generation**:
   - Vouchers must be generated by authorized issuers using the local command-line engine:
     ```powershell
     $env:LOCAL_MAC_SECRET="<high-entropy-operator-secret>"
     node tools/resilience/continuity-engine.mjs generate-voucher <roundId>
     ```
   - This writes a JSON payload containing a cryptographic `preimage`, a derived `nullifier` ($H(preimage)$), and a message authentication code (`mac`) binding the fields ([continuity-engine.mjs:L151-164](file:///c:/Users/Josh/Desktop/PBMRebateTreasuryFinal/tools/resilience/continuity-engine.mjs#L151-164)).
   
2. **Distribution**:
   - The JSON payload is printed as a physical card containing:
     - The JSON string formatted as a QR Code.
     - The plaintext `mac`, `roundId`, and `nullifier` prefix for manual fallback verification.
   
3. **Validation & Redemption**:
   - Upon presentation, the pharmacy uses the standalone air-gapped browser page `tools/offline/continuity-kit.html` ([continuity-kit.html:L229-252](file:///c:/Users/Josh/Desktop/PBMRebateTreasuryFinal/tools/offline/continuity-kit.html#L229-252)).
   - The pharmacist inputs their copy of the `LOCAL_MAC_SECRET`, uploads/pastes the voucher JSON, and runs the MAC check.
   - If the MAC is valid, the pharmacy accepts the paper voucher as a local debt obligation and dispenses the medication.

4. **Security & Key Management**:
   - **Shared Local Secrets**: The use of a shared `LOCAL_MAC_SECRET` creates a single point of compromise ([NEXT_REVIEW_HANDOFF.md:L319-320](file:///c:/Users/Josh/Desktop/PBMRebateTreasuryFinal/NEXT_REVIEW_HANDOFF.md#L319-320)). Keys must be rotated every 30 days.
   - **Zero PII**: Voter and pharmacy addresses must **never** be written to the paper voucher payload. This is tested in [ContinuityAndAdversarialTools.test.js:L52-75](file:///c:/Users/Josh/Desktop/PBMRebateTreasuryFinal/test/ContinuityAndAdversarialTools.test.js#L52-75).
   - **No On-Chain Settlement Authority**: Paper vouchers are *not* on-chain proofs. A verifier-signed mock ZK proof is still required before submission to [PBMRebateTreasury.sol](file:///c:/Users/Josh/Desktop/PBMRebateTreasuryFinal/contracts/PBMRebateTreasury.sol) or [PharmacyMutualCredit.sol](file:///c:/Users/Josh/Desktop/PBMRebateTreasuryFinal/contracts/PharmacyMutualCredit.sol).

### 3.2 Tabletop Scenario: Regional Telecom Blackout
* **Event**: A severe hurricane destroys cellular and fiber-optic backbones across District 4, cutting off 12 independent pharmacies from the internet for 14 days. Patients with insulin and heart medication regimens face immediate stockout risks.
* **Response Phase**:
  1. **Triage Mode Activation**: The Council triggers the emergency offline paper voucher protocol.
  2. **Local Key Distribution**: Operators distribute the current district `LOCAL_MAC_SECRET` to local coordinators via physical USB keys.
  3. **Voucher Printing**: Coordinators generate and print physical QR vouchers using a battery-powered laptop and thermal printer.
  4. **Ad-hoc Mutual Credit**: Pharmacies load `tools/offline/continuity-kit.html` from local USB storage onto offline pharmacy PCs. They verify the QR vouchers offline, register the nullifiers in physical paper logbooks, and dispense the drugs.
  5. **Post-Event Settlement**: Once telecom services are restored, the pharmacies package their paper logs into a batch via `package-relay` and submit them to the proxy verifier.
* **Recovery Tasks**:
  - Rotate the district `LOCAL_MAC_SECRET` immediately following reconnection.
  - Audit the paper logs against on-chain events using `reconcile-vouchers.mjs`.

## 4. Operational Playbook 2: SMS Verification & Fallback

### 4.1 Playbook Procedures
For patients without smartphones or areas where internet connections are down but cellular voice/SMS remains functional, SMS gateways act as transaction relays.

1. **Protocol Architecture**:
   - A trusted cooperative proxy runs an SMS gateway connected to a local GSM modem.
   - When a patient claims a voucher, the pharmacy/patient sends a structured SMS to the gateway:
     `CLAIM <roundId> <nullifier_prefix> <mac>`
   
2. **Proxy Authentication**:
   - The gateway validates the incoming message's syntax.
   - It verifies the MAC against the database of active `LOCAL_MAC_SECRET` keys.
   - If verified, the gateway marks the voucher status as `pending_sync` in its local offline queue.

3. **Privacy Protections**:
   - Under no circumstances should the patient's phone number, NPI (National Provider Identifier), diagnosis codes, or drug classifications be transmitted via SMS.
   - Gateways must purge SMS transaction logs from their local databases within 24 hours of batch packaging.

### 4.2 Tabletop Scenario: PBM Carrier Filtering & Network Audits
* **Event**: A major PBM detects high volumes of structured SMS transmissions originating from cooperative pharmacy numbers and commands the telecom provider to block or throttle the short-code numbers, simulating a denial-of-service attack on the SMS gateway.
* **Response Phase**:
  1. **Traffic Analysis**: The gateway operator detects a sudden drop in incoming SMS queue volume and a spike in routing errors.
  2. **Alternate Routing Protocol**: The system dynamically shifts to fallback long-code numbers or local mesh networks (e.g., LoRaWAN-to-SMS bridges).
  3. **Client-Side Obfuscation**: The system updates the SMS format to use randomized padding and base64 encoding to prevent basic pattern-matching by telecom filters.
  4. **Trusted Proxy Hand-off**: Physical proxies drive to adjacent cell towers outside the filtered zone to dump queued transactions.
* **Recovery Tasks**:
  - Update proxy software configurations to cycle target SMS gateway numbers automatically when throttling thresholds are crossed.

## 5. Operational Playbook 3: Delayed-Sync

### 5.1 Playbook Procedures
During prolonged periods of internet degradation, local databases queue transactions for batch upload at a later date.

1. **Batch Packaging**:
   - Pharmacies collect verified voucher JSONs in a local directory.
   - The directory is packaged using the continuity engine tool:
     ```powershell
     node tools/resilience/continuity-engine.mjs package-relay <vouchersDir> <outputPath>
     ```
   - This command strips all bearer preimages and voter identifiers to prevent leakage ([continuity-engine.mjs:L243-267](file:///c:/Users/Josh/Desktop/PBMRebateTreasuryFinal/tools/resilience/continuity-engine.mjs#L243-267)).

2. **Proxy Validation**:
   - Before submission to the network, the relay batch is validated through the proxy validator tool:
     ```powershell
     node tools/resilience/proxy-validator.js <outputPath>
     ```
   - The validator asserts that:
     - No forbidden fields (like `preimage`, `wallet`, or `voterAddress`) are present ([proxy-validator.js:L8-27](file:///c:/Users/Josh/Desktop/PBMRebateTreasuryFinal/tools/resilience/proxy-validator.js#L8-27)).
     - No duplicate nullifiers exist across the batch ([proxy-validator.js:L109-123](file:///c:/Users/Josh/Desktop/PBMRebateTreasuryFinal/tools/resilience/proxy-validator.js#L109-123)).

3. **Deterministic Reconciliation**:
   - Once reconnected, operators run the deterministic reconciliation tool:
     ```powershell
     node tools/resilience/reconcile-vouchers.mjs <vouchersPathOrDir> <onChainEventsJsonPath>
     ```
   - The tool classifies each voucher:
     - `reconciled`: Successfully matches an on-chain event ([reconcile-vouchers.mjs:L68-70](file:///c:/Users/Josh/Desktop/PBMRebateTreasuryFinal/tools/resilience/reconcile-vouchers.mjs#L68-70)).
     - `duplicate_conflict`: Multiple instances of the same nullifier are present ([reconcile-vouchers.mjs:L65-67](file:///c:/Users/Josh/Desktop/PBMRebateTreasuryFinal/tools/resilience/reconcile-vouchers.mjs#L65-67)).
     - `unresolved`: Voucher is local-only and has not yet been processed on-chain ([reconcile-vouchers.mjs:L62-63](file:///c:/Users/Josh/Desktop/PBMRebateTreasuryFinal/tools/resilience/reconcile-vouchers.mjs#L62-63)).

### 5.2 Tabletop Scenario: Reconnecting a Blacked-Out Cooperative
* **Event**: After a 10-day power and internet blackout, a cooperative of 5 pharmacies reconnects to the main network. They have a backlog of 350 offline vouchers.
* **Response Phase**:
  1. **Batch Assembly**: The local administrator collects all JSON vouchers from the 5 pharmacies onto a secure, air-gapped machine.
  2. **Preprocessing**: The admin runs `package-relay` to create the upload file `relay-batch-dist4.json`.
  3. **Local Validation**: The admin validates the file using `proxy-validator.js`. It passes, confirming no preimages are leaked.
  4. **Secure Transmission**: The batch file is uploaded to the cooperative's server proxy.
  5. **On-Chain Matching**: The system runs `reconcile-vouchers.mjs` against a dump of the treasury's on-chain events.
     - 342 vouchers are classified as `unresolved` (they are valid, new offline claims).
     - 8 vouchers return `duplicate_conflict` and are quarantined for investigation.
* **Recovery Tasks**:
  - The 342 valid vouchers are submitted for verifier-signed mock ZK attestations and submitted to the smart contracts.
  - The 8 conflicting vouchers are analyzed for double-redemption attempts or data entry errors.

## 6. Operational Playbook 4: Duplicate & Forged Receipts

### 6.1 Playbook Procedures
When offline claims are reconciled, malicious or negligent actors may attempt to submit duplicate or forged receipts to double-spend credit or drain treasury pools.

1. **Detection of Duplicates**:
   - The system checks for duplicates at three gates:
     - **Batch Generation**: `package-relay` fails if duplicate nullifiers are found in the local folder ([continuity-engine.mjs:L254-258](file:///c:/Users/Josh/Desktop/PBMRebateTreasuryFinal/tools/resilience/continuity-engine.mjs#L254-258)).
     - **Proxy Intake**: `proxy-validator.js` rejects files containing duplicate nullifiers ([proxy-validator.js:L117-120](file:///c:/Users/Josh/Desktop/PBMRebateTreasuryFinal/tools/resilience/proxy-validator.js#L117-120)).
     - **Reconciliation**: `reconcile-vouchers.mjs` marks duplicate nullifiers as `duplicate_conflict` ([reconcile-vouchers.mjs:L65-67](file:///c:/Users/Josh/Desktop/PBMRebateTreasuryFinal/tools/resilience/reconcile-vouchers.mjs#L65-67)).

2. **Detection of Forgeries**:
   - If a voucher payload is modified (e.g., changing the `roundId` or `preimage` to claim more credit), the MAC calculation will fail to match the signature.
   - The CLI tool `verify-offline` and browser kit will immediately return an `OFFLINE VERIFICATION FAILED` error, failing closed ([continuity-engine.mjs:L127-134](file:///c:/Users/Josh/Desktop/PBMRebateTreasuryFinal/tools/resilience/continuity-engine.mjs#L127-134), [continuity-kit.html:L236-238](file:///c:/Users/Josh/Desktop/PBMRebateTreasuryFinal/tools/offline/continuity-kit.html#L236-238)).

3. **Remediation & Triage**:
   - If a pharmacy is excluded from a claim due to a technical error (e.g., their voucher nullifier matches an existing on-chain nullifier from a different patient), they submit a dispute flag accompanied by participant testimony ([EVIDENCE_METADATA.md](file:///c:/Users/Josh/Desktop/PBMRebateTreasuryFinal/EVIDENCE_METADATA.md)).
   - Remediations are funded via the `exclusionRemediationReserve` in [PBMRebateTreasury.sol](file:///c:/Users/Josh/Desktop/PBMRebateTreasuryFinal/contracts/PBMRebateTreasury.sol) to avoid consuming standard Merkle distribution pools.
   - Payouts require `COUNCIL_ROLE` resolution, `ROOT_CONFIRMER_ROLE` approval, and cap checks ([PBMRebateTreasury.sol:L61-64](file:///c:/Users/Josh/Desktop/PBMRebateTreasuryFinal/contracts/PBMRebateTreasury.sol#L61-64)).

### 6.2 Tabletop Scenario: Exploit of Leaked MAC Secret
* **Event**: An operator's private laptop is stolen, leaking a district's active `LOCAL_MAC_SECRET`. A hostile actor generates 50 forged vouchers with custom nullifiers and tries to submit them through the proxy validator to claim mutual credit from the cooperative.
* **Response Phase**:
  1. **Anomalous Volume Alert**: The system alerts the Council to a surge in offline voucher submissions for the current epoch.
  2. **Proxy Rejection**: The proxy validator parses the incoming file. While individual MACs pass local checks, the validator detects that the nullifiers do not exist in any printed records or coordinator pre-allocation tables.
  3. **Revocation & Pause**: The Guardian calls `pause()` on [PharmacyMutualCredit.sol](file:///c:/Users/Josh/Desktop/PBMRebateTreasuryFinal/contracts/PharmacyMutualCredit.sol#L307-309) to halt all credit transfers and voucher redemptions.
  4. **Key Revocation**: The Council deauthorizes the compromised coordinator's address using `updateIssuerStatus(compromisedIssuer, false)` ([PharmacyMutualCredit.sol:L139-154](file:///c:/Users/Josh/Desktop/PBMRebateTreasuryFinal/contracts/PharmacyMutualCredit.sol#L139-154)).
  5. **Ledger Auditing**: Operators run `reconcile-vouchers.mjs` against the on-chain ledger to ensure no forged vouchers were successfully redeemed.
* **Recovery Tasks**:
  - Rotate the `LOCAL_MAC_SECRET` for all operators.
  - Implement a 30-day grace period for valid vouchers created before the issuer's deregistration timestamp ([PharmacyMutualCredit.sol:L226-235](file:///c:/Users/Josh/Desktop/PBMRebateTreasuryFinal/contracts/PharmacyMutualCredit.sol#L226-235)).
  - Once keys are rotated and the ledger is verified clean, the Council calls `unpause()` ([PharmacyMutualCredit.sol:L311-313](file:///c:/Users/Josh/Desktop/PBMRebateTreasuryFinal/contracts/PharmacyMutualCredit.sol#L311-313)).
